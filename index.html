About

Built for athletes.
Grounded in evidence.

I work with athletes, coaches and clubs across Ireland — helping them develop the mental skills that determine performance. My approach is practical, evidence-based, and built for the demands of real sport.

The CQ Perform suite brings that work into a digital format — accessible tools that athletes and coaches can use independently, between sessions, or as part of a wider performance programme.

The Tools

A suite built for
every stage of performance.

CQ Recover

The mental side of injury recovery. Three phases from just injured to return to play — covering identity, catastrophising, fear avoidance, visualisation and more.

Pre-Performance Routine

A structured pre-match mental routine. Checklists, cue words and visualisation — everything an athlete needs to arrive ready.

Player Reflection

Post-match reflection for individual players. Honest self-assessment of preparation, mindset and intensity — building the habit of learning from every game.

Post-match reflection for coaches. A structured review of decisions, communication and leadership — because better coaches build better teams.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Who do you work with?

GAA teams and players at club and county level, hurling and football.

Where are you based?

Ballincollig, Cork. I work in person across Cork and further afield by arrangement.

What does working with a team look like?

Anything from a single squad workshop to being embedded with the panel for a full season, which is how I worked with Ballincollig on the way to the 2026 county title.

Do you work with individual players?

Yes. One-to-one work is available, and players on teams I work with can access it within the season.

Do you have tools teams can use?

Yes. I've built apps for player reflection, pre-game routines, injury recovery and coach reflection. They're in The Tools above, and we can talk about how they'd fit your team.
